# Brown-Driver-Briggs: provenance

Module `eng_bdb`, abbreviation BDB.

## The work

Francis Brown, with the co-operation of S. R. Driver and Charles A. Briggs,
*A Hebrew and English Lexicon of the Old Testament, with an appendix
containing the Biblical Aramaic, based on the Lexicon of William Gesenius as
translated by Edward Robinson* (Oxford: Clarendon Press, 1906). Public
domain.

Reference scan used for every check below: Internet Archive
`hebrewenglishlex00browuoft` (University of Toronto copy of the 1906
printing); printed page p is image n{p+23}, give or take two.

## Sources

- **The text: Sefaria's "BDB Dictionary" and "BDB Aramaic Dictionary"**
  (11,015 + 863 entries), marked public domain by Sefaria, whose version
  source is the National Library of Israel's record of the 1906 edition
  (`NNL_ALEPH990019502310205171`). Sefaria doesn't say who transcribed it.
  It reads as OCR that was then corrected and edited: the English is clean,
  while the Hebrew has the slips OCR makes (letters that look alike, small
  superscript numbers misread). Its editors spelled out BDB's
  abbreviations, wrote references in full ("Je 26:1" for Je 26¹), and added
  links; that editing is kept. Locked copy: `source/bdb.json`, extracted
  from Sefaria's public database dump by `tools/extract-sefaria-bdb.py`
  (details and hashes in `source/SOURCE.md`).
- **Strong's numbers: the Open Scriptures Hebrew Bible Project's lexical
  index** (`source/oshb/LexicalIndex.xml`, HebrewLexicon commit `21c9add`,
  2019-09-02), licensed CC BY 4.0, which gives Strong's numbers entry by
  entry. Sefaria's own numbers are given to whole groups of related entries
  (3,299 numbers on more than one entry), so the index is followed wherever
  it places a number (7,997 numbers) and Sefaria's are kept for the rest
  (432). `strongs-review.md` lists every difference from Sefaria's: 92
  numbers moved to other entries, 17 partly moved, 1,123 narrowed to fewer
  entries, 343 added. Seven checked against Strong's dictionary all
  favoured the index (`print-checks.md`).
- **The check text: the Westminster Leningrad Codex**, eBible.org's
  `hboWLC` release (`assets/hbo_wlc/ebible/`, PGP signature checked), used
  only to check BDB's Hebrew quotations, never as BDB's text.

## Conversion

`tools/import-sefaria-bdb.py` writes `eng_bdb.tei.xml` (TEI P5, following
TEI Lex-0; its header describes the encoding). The independent
`tools/check-sefaria-bdb.py`, which shares no code with it, confirms that
every entry's text, headword line, senses and links match the source with
the corrections made. Left out: the GK and TWOT numbers (numbering systems
still under copyright), Sefaria's empty quotation lists and links between
neighbouring headwords, and the links (not the text) of the 118 references
to Ben Sira and Maccabees.

## Checking the Hebrew quotations

`tools/check-bdb-quotations.py` compares the 41,842 Hebrew quotations that
stand directly before a reference to one verse with that verse in the WLC.
56.5% match exactly and 15.6% except for a prefix BDB leaves off; most of
the rest are BDB quoting a dictionary form, a Qere for a Ketiv, Baer's
spelling (BDB used Baer's edition, not the Leningrad Codex), or its own
proposed reading. These are kept as printed. The maintainer checked
samples of each kind in the 1906 print (`print-checks.md`).

## Corrections

Every correction is marked in the TEI file (`choice` with `sic` and `corr`,
`bn:meant`, or a `change` in the header) and listed in `corrections.yaml`
and `corrections-letters.yaml`:

- **313 one-letter slips in the Hebrew** (he for het, bet for kaf, dalet
  for resh and the like), corrected from the WLC: only the one letter
  changes, Sefaria's vowel points are kept. In a sample of 12 checked in
  the print, 11 were Sefaria's slips and one was BDB's own misprint, kept
  as printed with a note. Generated by `tools/make-bdb-letter-corrections.py`
  from the quotation report, so no Hebrew is typed by hand.
- **6 references** Sefaria transcribed wrongly, restored from the print
  (one where Sefaria had given the English verse number, Ez 20:49 for BDB's
  Ez 21⁵).
- **12 references BDB itself misprinted** (11, and Ne 7⁷⁰, where BDB counts
  a verse the Leningrad Codex lacks): kept as printed, and linked to the
  verse meant.
- **2 homograph numbers** (I. and II. olah, "whole burnt-offering" and "ascent", which Sefaria
  numbers II. and III.).
- **1 occurrence count** Sefaria split ("c. 6823", for the divine name).
- **6 markup slips** in Sefaria's HTML (tags only; the wording is
  unchanged).

## Known limits

- Sefaria's homograph numbers (`ordinal`) can't be checked automatically,
  because BDB numbers homographs by root and skips numbers on purpose.
  They are kept as Sefaria has them, corrected where found wrong.
- 149 Hebrew passages in 57 entries are empty in Sefaria's text (the Hebrew
  was lost in transcription). They are empty in this module too, until
  they are restored from the print.
- The 148 quotations that fit a neighbouring verse better than the one
  cited are left as printed: a sample were BDB's own numbering.
- References follow BDB's printed numbering, which is the Hebrew Bible's
  (and so the WLC's), except where BDB follows Baer's edition.
